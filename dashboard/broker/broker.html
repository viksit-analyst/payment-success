Link your Upstox account to enable live execution. Viksit Analyst never sees your password — authentication happens directly on upstox.com.

Enable automatic daily reconnection

Upstox logs every account out at ~3:30 AM daily. Without this, you'd need to manually reconnect every trading morning before 9 AM for your bot to place trades. Enabling this stores your Upstox login details, encrypted, so the platform can log back in on your behalf each morning. This means Viksit Analyst will have your Upstox TOTP secret and PIN on file, encrypted at rest. Only enable this if you're comfortable with that trade-off.
