What Is Viksit Analyst

Algo trading, without the guesswork of a signal service.

Viksit Analyst is not a tips or signal-selling service - it's the layer that turns quantitative research into fully automated trades. Here's what actually happens between a market observation and an order in your account.

Research & Strategy

Each strategy - IVRV, Gamma Flip and VWAP - starts as a research question, is tested against historical data, and is only promoted to live trading once it passes internal review. A market idea becomes a defined, rules-based strategy before it ever sees your capital.

Validation, Not Guesswork

Before any order is placed, every signal passes through risk validation, a subscription check, broker validation and a margin check. If any step fails, no trade happens - there's no override and no manual step for you to get wrong.

Execution & Reporting

Orders that pass validation are placed automatically through your connected broker - nothing is sent to you to act on manually. After the session closes, you receive a report summarizing what happened, not a signal for you to interpret.
