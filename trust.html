Risk Architecture

Every trade passes four checks before it's placed.

This sequence runs the same way, every time, for every order, regardless of which of the three strategies generated it. You don't see the signal that triggered a trade - you can see that it was checked.

1. Risk Validation

The signal is checked against position sizing, exposure, and portfolio-level risk rules before anything else happens.

2. Subscription Check

The system confirms your subscription to that specific strategy is active before an order can be generated on your behalf.

3. Broker Validation

Your broker connection is verified as live and authorized via OAuth before any order is routed to it.

4. Margin Check

Available margin in your own broker account is confirmed as sufficient before the order is placed - never before.

Every gate pass, timestamp, and order is logged automatically and cannot be edited after the fact.

Non-Custodial Architecture

Your capital never leaves your own broker account.

Viksit Analyst connects to your broker through OAuth - the industry-standard method that grants a platform permission to place orders without ever holding your login credentials or your funds. The ₹9,999 activation and ₹999/month subscription are fees paid to Viksit Analyst for the platform; your recommended ₹3,00,000+ trading capital stays exactly where it already is, in your own account, at your own broker, at all times.

If a Strategy Underperforms

Strategies are reviewed against their own research expectations on an ongoing basis. If a strategy's live behavior diverges meaningfully from what its research indicated it should do, it can be paused for review rather than left running unexamined. Any such pause, and the reasoning behind it in plain language, would be reflected in the Research log and on Mission Control - not left unsaid.
